First Invention is a year-long, hands-on chapter program where high-school students learn engineering, electronics, coding, and prototyping by making things that actually work.

First Invention is in its founding stage. The program, chapter model, and resources on this site are being built and refined before broader launch.

What First Invention is

Not another STEM club that only talks about ideas.

Students learn the fundamentals, use them immediately, then grow toward a real invention with evidence behind it.

01

Learn

Explore structures, motion, circuits, breadboards, sensors, coding, and design thinking through quick, interactive challenges.

02

Build

Use materials, tools, microcontrollers, and teamwork to make functioning devices instead of only watching demonstrations.

03

Invent

Choose a real problem, prototype a solution, test it honestly, improve it, and explain what the evidence shows.

The full-year experience

Thirty 45-minute meetings. One growing set of real skills.

Each chapter follows a September-to-May progression designed to fit a school lunch, advisory, or club period.

01

Become a Maker

Tools, safety, structures, forces, and mechanisms.

02

Circuits & Electronics

LEDs, breadboards, inputs, sensors, sound, light, and motion.

03

Physical Computing

Microcontrollers, code, responsive devices, and debugging.

04

Invent Something Real

Problem research, prototypes, testing, iteration, and pitching.

05

Teach & Lead

Teaching Fellow preparation and supervised Outreach Labs.

Three moments to show the work

Build. Test. Document. Explain.

Three internal chapter challenges create shared milestones across the year. Every challenge values a working build, honest testing, and clear explanation.

FALL

Build Sprint

Design and test a mechanical solution for a simple mission.

WINTER

Circuit Challenge

Make a useful device that turns an input into an output.

SPRING

First Invention Challenge

Develop, test, and pitch a real problem-focused invention.
