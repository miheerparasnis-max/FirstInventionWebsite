Build what matters.

First Invention is building a national network of hands-on STEM chapters where high-school students learn engineering, electronics, coding, and prototyping by making real things.

Start a Founding ChapterExplore the Program

First Invention is developing its founding curriculum and chapter model. The pages on this site describe the program we are building and include clearly marked placeholders.

The idea

From first build to real invention.

Build

Learn practical STEM skills through circuits, sensors, code, structures, prototypes, and testing.

Invent

Use those skills to turn a real problem into a documented, working solution.

Teach

Become a Teaching Fellow and lead supervised invention workshops for younger students.

Year one

A full school year of hands-on STEM.

Thirty interactive meetings, three online challenges, and a final invention portfolio. Full lesson packages and chapter resources are planned for the founding curriculum.
