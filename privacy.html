Privacy

This is a placeholder notice, not the final First Invention privacy policy.

What the current site does

The current site is informational. Its displayed interest and contact forms are disabled and do not intentionally collect, store, or transmit submissions.

What must happen before forms open

First Invention will publish a complete privacy policy, establish a domain email, define secure data handling, and confirm the appropriate student and family-permission process.

Future policy topics

The final policy will explain what information is collected, why it is needed, how it is stored and protected, how long it is retained, who can access it, how questions or deletion requests can be made, and how student information is handled.
