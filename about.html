About First Invention

Every young person deserves the chance to become an inventor.

First Invention is building a national hands-on STEM chapter model for high-school students.

Our mission

First Invention equips young people with the technical skills, tools, and confidence to turn real problems into working inventions.

Frequently asked questions

Is First Invention a nonprofit?

Not yet. This website does not claim nonprofit status or tax-exempt status.

Who is the program for?

The founding chapter model is designed for high-school students. The future Teaching Fellow program brings supervised hands-on STEM workshops to younger students.

When will chapters open?

The founding curriculum, operations system, and launch process are currently in development.

How can I get in touch?

A First Invention domain email will be added before the public launch. This site currently does not collect contact information.
