About First Invention

Every student deserves a real first build.

First Invention is a founding-stage hands-on STEM chapter model for high-school students who want to learn by making.

Our mission

First Invention equips young people with the technical skills, tools, and confidence to turn real problems into working inventions.

Why this exists

Too many students are told to be innovative before they have ever used a breadboard, tested a mechanism, written a line of code, or seen an idea fail productively. First Invention starts with those practical moments.

Who it serves

The core chapter experience is for high-school students. Trained Teaching Fellows may later lead supervised Outreach Labs for younger students with an adult host and the right permissions.

Founding-stage promise

Grow carefully. Support chapters well.

First Invention is being built through a small number of well-supported chapters, not by claiming scale before the system is ready. The goal is a program students can actually run, advisors can support, and schools can trust.

Frequently asked questions

Is First Invention a nonprofit?

Not yet. This website does not claim nonprofit status or tax-exempt status.

Who is the program for?

The founding chapter model is designed for high-school students. Supervised Outreach Labs are a separate future pathway for younger students.

Are chapters open now?

The curriculum, operations system, and launch process are being developed. A small founding cohort will come before a broader launch.

Can I contact First Invention?

A domain email and secure contact workflow will be added before the public launch. The current site does not collect contact information.
