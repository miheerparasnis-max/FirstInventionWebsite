Founding chapters

Bring hands-on invention to your school.

First Invention is preparing a small founding cohort of high-school chapters that will help test, improve, and set the standard for the program.

Who can start one?

A committed student leadership team and an adult advisor who can support school approval, meeting supervision, safety, and communication.

What a chapter needs

A regular 45-minute meeting time, a safe school-approved space, a small core group of high-school members, and a willingness to document what works.

The expected path

Recruit a core team

Bring together students who are ready to build, lead, and keep the chapter consistent.

Find an advisor

Ask a faculty or staff member to provide school support and meeting supervision.

Get school approval

Complete your school’s club-approval process before any meeting or outreach activity begins.

Apply to the founding cohort

Use the official form once it opens, then complete onboarding and readiness checks.

Student leadership

Chapter Lead

Keeps the meeting plan, team goals, and chapter communication on track.

Technical Lead

Helps prepare builds, inventory materials, and guide safe technical troubleshooting.

Operations Lead

Supports attendance, documentation, meeting logistics, and the chapter handoff.

What First Invention plans to provide

A 30-lesson curriculum, Team Invention Portfolio, advisor and safety handbooks, maker-kit guidance, competition resources, Teaching Fellow training, and chapter operations templates.
