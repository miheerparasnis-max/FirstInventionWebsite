Year one curriculum

A year of building, not just talking about ideas.

The planned September-to-May program contains 30 interactive meetings, with space for local holidays, exams, challenges, and showcases.

1. Become a Maker

Lab safety, tools, structures, mechanisms, and the Fall Build Sprint.

2. Circuits and Electronics

Voltage, breadboards, buttons, sensors, outputs, and a Circuit Remix Challenge.

3. Physical Computing

Microcontrollers, code, responsive devices, debugging, and the Winter Prototype Challenge.

4. Invent Something Real

Problem research, solution planning, prototype sprints, testing, and a final portfolio.

5. Teach, Showcase, and Lead

Teaching Fellow preparation, outreach labs, showcase planning, and chapter handoff.

Chapter Resource Library

Lesson plans, slides, student workbook pages, maker-kit lists, safety guides, and videos will live here.
