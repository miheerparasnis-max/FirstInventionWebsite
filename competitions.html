Internal challenges + future opportunities

Build it. Test it. Explain what happened.

Three internal chapter challenges give every team a clear milestone. The goal is not a polished idea alone; it is evidence of building, testing, iteration, and explanation.

Fall

Build Sprint

Teams create a mechanical solution to a defined mission using accessible materials and document what they tested.

Winter

Circuit Challenge

Teams build a useful input-to-output device, explain the wiring or code, and show its behavior through testing evidence.

Spring

First Invention Challenge

Teams research a problem, build a prototype, test it honestly, and make a concise evidence-based pitch.

The submission standard

What teams show

A working build or honest prototype, diagrams or design notes, test evidence, a clear explanation, and a record of what changed.

What reviewers value

Technical function, thoughtful design, testing and iteration, accurate documentation, and clear communication - not expensive materials or outside access.
