For educators

You do not need to be an engineer to help students become inventors.

Advisors make the chapter possible: they provide adult supervision, support student leaders, help with school requirements, and keep the program safe and consistent.

What advisors do

  • Supervise meetings, materials, and safety.
  • Serve as the school liaison and support approval.
  • Help students keep a reliable meeting rhythm.
  • Support student leaders without running the club for them.
  • Ensure adult oversight and host requirements for Outreach Labs.

What advisors do not need to do

  • Be an engineer, programmer, or electronics expert.
  • Teach every technical concept from scratch.
  • Buy materials personally or create every lesson.
  • Run unsupervised outreach or manage student data outside school policy.

Time commitment

A consistent adult presence, not a second full-time job.

The expected chapter rhythm is one 45-minute meeting each week, plus light preparation and school communication. Student officers should lead the meeting; the advisor keeps the environment safe, supported, and accountable.
