For educators

You do not need to be an engineer to help students become inventors.

Advisors support student leaders, provide supervision, help navigate school requirements, and keep meetings safe and consistent.

The advisor role

  • Supervise meetings and safety.
  • Serve as school liaison.
  • Help secure meeting space and approvals.
  • Support student leaders without running the club for them.
  • Ensure appropriate adult oversight for outreach.
